## Strategic Value

GDF enables a new class of AI infrastructure:

- **Policy-Governed AI Platforms**  
  AI systems that can prove, in real time, that every action complies with enterprise and regulatory constraints.

- **Interoperable Governance**  
  Enterprises can change AI models, vendors, or deployment environments without rebuilding their compliance stack.

- **Regulation as Code**  
  Legal and compliance requirements become machine-enforceable contracts, not documentation exercises.

- **AI-Native SIEM and GRC Integration**  
  GDF decisions can be ingested directly into security, risk, and governance platforms as first-class events.
