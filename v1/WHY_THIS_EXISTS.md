Why GDF Exists (Refined)

As AI systems move from advisory tools to autonomous actors, traditional logging and observability systems fail to answer the questions regulators, auditors, and executives now demand:

Who authorized this AI to act?

What data was it permitted to access?

Under which regulation and policy was that decision governed?

What would have happened if this policy had not existed?

The Guardrail Decision Format (GDF) exists to transform AI behavior into a portable, auditable, and machine-verifiable governance artifact — not just an application log or model trace.

GDF is designed to function as:

A compliance record for regulators and internal audit

A forensic artifact for security and incident response teams

A telemetry signal for AI platforms and cloud infrastructure

A governance contract between AI vendors, enterprises, and oversight bodies
