## Why GDF Exists

As AI systems move from advisory tools to autonomous actors, traditional logging and observability systems fail to answer the questions regulators, auditors, and executives actually ask:

- Who authorized this AI to act?
- What data was it allowed to access?
- What regulation governed that decision?
- What would have happened if this policy had not existed?

The Guardrail Decision Format (GDF) exists to turn AI behavior into a **portable, auditable, and machine-verifiable governance artifact** — not just a log entry.

GDF is designed to function as:
- A compliance record for regulators
- A forensic artifact for security teams
- A telemetry signal for AI platforms
- A governance contract between AI vendors and enterprises
